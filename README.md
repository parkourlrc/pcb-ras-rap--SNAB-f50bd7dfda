# Switch iN A Bottle (SNAB)

SNAB is a protable device that allows you to take full control of your switch.

## What does this do?

- RCM payload injection
- Bluetooth gamepad dongle
- Charge monitoring
- Maybe more

## Why did you make this?

My switch dies quite often when I travel, and I dont always have some kind of computer avalible, and I use an iPhone so I cant use my phone. Also I wanted to learn how the Fusee Gelee exploit works and how to design hardware projects.

## Why not buy a commecial product on Amazon?

There's nothing stopping you from doing that, but sometimes it's more fun to do it yourself, plus you learn something along the way.
